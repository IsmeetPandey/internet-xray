# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Contributors and users are expected to communicate respectfully, assume good faith, and focus criticism on ideas and code rather than people.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive and specific.
- Welcome questions and different technical perspectives.
- Respect privacy and do not share personal or sensitive data.
- Keep project discussions relevant to the repository.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, deliberate disruption, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when behavior violates this standard. Serious concerns should be reported privately through the repository's security/contact channel.
